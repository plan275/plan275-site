Features

Mix up to four sounds

  • Play up to four sounds together.
  • Adjust the volume of each sound and the overall volume.
  • Sounds loop without gaps.
  • Playback continues when you leave the app or turn off the screen. Play and pause from the lock screen.

Favorites

  • Save up to five mixes.
  • Switch to a saved mix with one tap.
  • The app restores your last mix when you open it again.

Sleep timer

  • Stops playback after 3 minutes to 12 hours.
  • Check the time left or turn the timer off at any time.

Landscape and tablets

  • Turn your phone sideways and the sounds and the mixer appear side by side.
  • They also appear side by side on Android tablets.

Foldables and iPhone Duo

On devices that fold, Sararak splits the screen at the fold, so no buttons or text sit on the crease.

iPhone Duo

  • Unfolded, the sounds sit on the left of the fold and the mixer on the right.
  • Unfolded and held upright, the sounds sit above the fold and the mixer below it.
  • Folded, it looks the same as on any iPhone.

Android foldables

  • Unfolded, the sounds and the mixer appear side by side.
  • Half-folded in tabletop posture, the sounds sit above the fold and the mixer below it.

Playback keeps going when you fold, unfold, or rotate the device.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection?
No. All sounds are included in the app, so they play even in airplane mode.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone (including iPhone Duo) with iOS 26 or later, and Android phones, tablets, and foldables with Android 13 or later.
Does it work on foldables and iPhone Duo?
Yes. When unfolded, the sounds and the mixer are split at the fold. When folded, it looks the same as on a regular phone.
Does it keep playing while I use other apps?
Playback continues when you leave the app or turn off the screen. If another app plays sound (a video, music, or a call), Sararak stops.
How many sounds can I mix at once?
Up to four. You can set the volume of each one.
Do I need an account?
No. Your favorites and your last mix are stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
