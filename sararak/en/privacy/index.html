Sararak Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how plan275 ("we", "us") handles information in Sararak, an ambient sound app for iOS and Android (the "App").

  • Effective date: 2026-10-20
  • Version: 1.0

Summary

  • The App has no sign-up or login. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • To improve the App, we collect app usage statistics through Google's Firebase Analytics. These statistics are tied to a random identifier created when you install the App, not to your name or contact details.
  • The App shows no ads, does not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA or Android Advertising ID), and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • Your favorites and your last sound mix are stored only on your device.

1. Information we collect

1.1 App usage statistics (collected automatically)

When you use the App, the Firebase Analytics SDK automatically collects the following and sends it to Google.

CategoryData
IdentifierApp instance ID (a random value created when the App is installed; it changes if you delete and reinstall the App)
Device and app informationDevice model, operating system and version, App version, device and App language, screen resolution
Approximate locationCountry, region, and city, estimated from your IP address. Google states that it does not store the IP addresses used for this estimate
App activityApp launches, session start and engagement time, screen views (Sounds, Favorites, Settings, Open source licenses), selecting and removing sounds, play and pause, volume changes, saving, applying and deleting favorites, starting, finishing and cancelling the sleep timer, and links opened in Settings
  • Sounds are recorded only by the identifier of a sound included in the App (for example, forest_after_rain). The App has no free text input.
  • The App records screen views itself; Firebase's automatic screen reporting is turned off.

1.2 Feedback email

"Send feedback" in the App opens your email app. If you send us an email, we receive:

  • Your email address, and the content and attachments of your message

1.3 Information we do not process

  • Purchase information: Purchases and payments are handled by the Apple App Store and Google Play. We do not receive your name, payment method, or card details. We see only sales statistics provided by the stores (such as the number of sales and countries).
  • Information stored only on your device: Your favorites (sound combinations and volumes) and your last mix stay on your device and are never sent to us.
    • This information may be included in your device backups (such as iCloud) on iOS, and in Google backup and device transfer on Android if you have turned them on. These backups are stored in your Apple or Google account, and we cannot see them.

2. How we use information

We do not use your information for any other purpose. If the purpose changes, we will let you know in advance and ask for consent where required.

3. How long we keep information

InformationRetention period
App usage statistics: individual event records2 months from collection
App usage statistics: user-level data linked to the app instance ID14 months from the last time you used the App. The period restarts whenever you use the App again. We keep it this long to compare usage across the year
App usage statistics: aggregated reportsTotals that cannot identify individuals (such as daily user counts) may be kept without a time limit
Feedback email1 year after your inquiry is resolved

Google Analytics deletes event records and user-level data automatically when these periods end.

4. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. We may disclose information when required by law, such as a valid request from a public authority.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us to access, correct, delete, or stop processing your personal information by emailing the contact below. We will respond without undue delay.
  • Because app usage statistics are not linked to your name or contact details, we may not be able to find records that belong to you. If so, we will tell you.
  • Deleting the App removes your favorites, last mix, and app instance ID from your device. If you reinstall it, a new app instance ID is created and is not linked to earlier statistics.
  • To stop the collection of usage statistics, turn off Settings > Privacy > Share usage statistics in the App. From then on, the App stops collecting and sending usage statistics and deletes the app instance ID and any unsent statistics from your device. All features keep working. Statistics already sent are deleted when the retention period in Section 3 ends. If you turn it back on, a new app instance ID is created.

7. Security

  • We collect only the minimum usage statistics needed and have turned off advertising identifier collection.
  • Usage statistics and emails are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Only the operator can access the Firebase console and the feedback mailbox, and both use two-step verification.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 14 (under 13 in the United States), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Region-specific information

California residents

  • In the past 12 months we collected the categories described in Section 1: identifiers (app instance ID), internet or other electronic network activity (app activity), and approximate geolocation. We collect them from your device through the App, for the purposes in Section 2.
  • We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • You may request access to or deletion of your information as described in Section 6. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
  • Do Not Track: The App does not respond to Do Not Track signals, because it does not track you across third-party apps or websites.

European Union, EEA and United Kingdom

The App is not offered in the European Union, the European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom.
